Chapter I — The Vision
The Vision

Cities that see their water

VarunNetra, meaning "Water's Eye", is an integrated framework that brings AI vision, IoT sensing, and sustainable design into one connected urban water intelligence system. Built by two students from Haryana.

Unlike fragmented approaches that tackle drainage, supply, or harvesting in isolation, VarunNetra unifies them into a closed-loop ecosystem that is predictive, responsive, and scalable.

Artificial Intelligence IoT Sensors Rainwater Harvesting Smart Drainage Real-time Monitoring Citizen Platform
Chapter II — Three Pillars
Three Modules

The three pillars of VarunNetra

Each module solves a different water challenge, but the real strength is how they work together as one closed-loop system.

01 / 03
AI Smart Drainage

MeghPath

Computer vision continuously monitors drainage inlets, detects waste, and removes blockage risks before waterlogging builds.

Computer Vision DetectionCamera images classify waste by shape, density, and obstruction level.
Automated RemovalDC motors and a conveyor arm respond instantly without manual intervention.
Corrosion-Resistant PVCFiltration mesh traps waste while preserving continuous water flow.
60–80%Reduction in drain blockages
02 / 03
IoT Pipeline Monitor

Jal Setu

Embeds pressure and pH sensors in pipelines, sending live readings through NodeMCU to a Python dashboard for anomaly detection.

Inline Pressure + pH SensorsESP8266 NodeMCU collects and transmits real-time pipeline data via Wi-Fi.
Python Monitoring DashboardLive visualization supports threshold alerts and faster decision-making.
Early Leak DetectionPressure drops and pH shifts reveal leaks, illegal tapping, or contamination.
30–40%Reduction in water losses
03 / 03
Rainwater Harvesting

Jal Stambh

Captures rainwater vertically, filters through gravel, sand, and activated carbon, and stores it for non-potable reuse while supporting birds.

Multi-Layer FiltrationGravel → Sand → Activated Carbon progressively removes impurities.
Decentralized ReuseStored water supports cleaning, sanitation, and irrigation use cases.
Biodiversity SupportBird feeder outlets are integrated directly into the structure.
15–25%Reduction in freshwater demand
Chapter III — What Testing Revealed
Testing Impact

What the testing showed

Early prototype results show why integrated water intelligence outperforms isolated single-problem solutions.

60–80%

Drain blockage reduction across plastic, paper, and organic debris tests through automated response.

40–50%

Estimated reduction in urban flooding risk from better drainage continuity and fewer reactive cycles.

30–40%

Water loss reduction through leak and illegal-tapping detection using pressure anomalies and pH deviations.

15–25%

Freshwater demand reduction through local rainwater reuse for non-potable applications.

24–72h

Maximum response time to water-system issues — significantly faster than manual inspection cycles.

3-in-1

Integrated AI + IoT + Rainwater system in one closed-loop framework instead of siloed solutions.

Chapter IV — How It All Connects
Architecture

How it all connects

Three physical modules feed one intelligence layer, while citizen participation closes the loop.

MeghPath — AI Camera ModuleComputer vision detects waste, triggers the mechanical response, and keeps drains clear.
Jal Setu — IoT Sensor ArrayPressure and pH sensors feed NodeMCU, Wi-Fi transmission, and anomaly detection.
Jal Stambh — Harvesting UnitRainwater is filtered, stored, and reused for non-potable demand reduction.
Python Dashboard + Varun SaathiCentralized monitoring, alerts, and citizen complaint integration create a full feedback loop.
